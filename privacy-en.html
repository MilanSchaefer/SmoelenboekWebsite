Privacy Statement

This statement explains which personal data is processed in the team directory and why.

What data do we process?

The following personal data may be displayed:

  • First and last name
  • Profile photo
  • Job title
  • Team
  • Location
  • Email address
  • Hobbies and interests

Why do we process this data?

The data is used to introduce employees within the organization and make it easier for colleagues to find each other.

Consent

Personal data and photos are only published when the relevant employee has given permission.

Storage and deletion

Data is not stored for longer than necessary for the team directory. When an employee no longer participates in the project, their data can be removed.

Questions

If you have questions about privacy or the information on this website, contact the person responsible for the project.
